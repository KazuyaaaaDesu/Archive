# Capstone 1 Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow describes the first phase of the capstone lifecycle. During Capstone 1, student groups develop and refine their research proposal through adviser consultations, document reviews, and the Proposal Defense.

The objective of this phase is to complete the proposal manuscript consisting of **Chapters 1, 2, and 3**, obtain adviser approval for each chapter, and successfully pass the Proposal Defense.

---

# Actors

- Student (Capstone Group)
- Adviser
- Coordinator
- Panelist

---

# Preconditions

Before this workflow begins:

- The capstone group has been successfully formed.
- An adviser has been assigned to the group.
- The group has access to the milestone workspace.
- The Capstone 1 milestone is open.

# Stage 1 – Topic Proposal

The capstone group proposes three research titles or topics.

After consultations and evaluation, the group selects one topic as the official capstone project.

Once the topic has been selected, it becomes the group's official research topic.

Although the research title may be refined during future consultations, the selected research topic cannot be changed.

---

# Stage 2 – Chapter Development

The group develops the following chapters:

- Chapter 1
- Chapter 2
- Chapter 3

Each chapter follows the same adviser consultation and review workflow.

The adviser may:

- Approve the submission, or
- Request revisions.

Students continue revising and resubmitting the chapter until it receives adviser approval.

---

# Stage 3 – Proposal Defense Eligibility

The group becomes eligible for the Proposal Defense only after:

- Chapter 1 has been approved.
- Chapter 2 has been approved.
- Chapter 3 has been approved.

---

# Stage 4 – Proposal Defense

After satisfying all Capstone 1 requirements, the group proceeds to the Proposal Defense.

The Proposal Defense workflow, including scheduling, panel evaluation, annotations, verdicts, and post-defense revisions, is documented separately in the **Proposal Defense Workflow**.

---

# Postconditions

After successful completion:

- Chapters 1, 2, and 3 have been approved.
- The Proposal Defense has been successfully completed.
- All required revisions have been addressed and approved.
- The Capstone 1 milestone is marked as completed.

---

# Business Notes

- Previous chapter approval is not required to begin the next chapter.
- All chapters must be approved before the Proposal Defense.
- Students may revise a chapter multiple times until it is approved.
- The selected research topic cannot be changed after confirmation.
- The project title may still be refined during adviser consultations.
- Proposal Defense is available only after all required chapters have been approved.
- Capstone 2 cannot begin until the Capstone 1 milestone has been completed.
