# Capstone 2 Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow describes the second phase of the capstone lifecycle. During Capstone 2, capstone groups complete the remaining research manuscript by developing **Chapters 4 and 5**, undergo adviser consultations and document reviews, and conclude the project through the Final Defense.

The objective of this phase is to complete the final capstone manuscript, successfully pass the Final Defense, and submit the approved manuscript for archiving.

---

# Actors

* Student (Capstone Group)
* Adviser
* Coordinator
* Panelist

---

# Preconditions

Before this workflow begins:

* The Capstone 1 milestone has been successfully completed.
* The Proposal Defense has been approved.
* The Coordinator has opened the Capstone 2 milestone.
* The capstone group has an assigned adviser.

---

# Workflow Overview

```text
Capstone 1 Completed
        │
        ▼
Coordinator Opens Capstone 2
        │
        ▼
Section Code Updated
        │
        ▼
Chapter 4 Development
        │
        ▼
Adviser Review
        │
        ▼
Chapter 5 Development
        │
        ▼
Adviser Review
        │
        ▼
All Chapters Approved
        │
        ▼
Final Defense
        │
        ▼
Archive Submission
```

---

# Stage 1 – Capstone 2 Activation

The Coordinator opens the Capstone 2 milestone for the class section.

Upon activation:

* The section advances to the next academic level.
* The section code is updated (e.g., **3BG1 → 4BG1**).
* The Capstone 2 milestone becomes available to all eligible groups.

---

# Stage 2 – Chapter Development

The capstone group develops:

* Chapter 4
* Chapter 5

Each chapter follows the standard **Adviser Review Workflow**, where the adviser reviews submissions, provides feedback, requests revisions when necessary, and approves completed chapters.

Students may continue developing subsequent chapters while previous chapters are under review.

---

# Stage 3 – Final Defense Eligibility

The group becomes eligible for the Final Defense after:

* Chapter 4 has been approved.
* Chapter 5 has been approved.

---

# Stage 4 – Final Defense

Once all Capstone 2 requirements have been completed, the group proceeds to the Final Defense.

The Final Defense follows the same scheduling, evaluation, annotation, verdict, and revision process as the Proposal Defense.

---

# Stage 5 – Archive Submission

After successfully completing the Final Defense:

1. The group submits the approved final capstone manuscript.

2. The system archives the manuscript.

3. The archived capstone project becomes available in the Repository as a reference for future students.

---

# Postconditions

After successful completion:

* Chapters 4 and 5 have been approved.
* The Final Defense has been successfully completed.
* The final manuscript has been archived.
* The capstone project is marked as completed.
* The archived manuscript becomes available in the Repository.

---

# Business Notes

* Only the Coordinator may open the Capstone 2 milestone.
* Opening Capstone 2 updates the section code to the next academic level.
* Students may continue developing subsequent chapters while previous chapters are under review.
* All required chapters must be approved before the Final Defense.
* The Final Defense follows the same workflow as the Proposal Defense.
* Only approved manuscripts may be archived.
