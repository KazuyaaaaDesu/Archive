# Final Defense Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow describes the Final Defense process for Capstone 2. The Final Defense follows the same scheduling, evaluation, annotation, verdict, and revision process as the Proposal Defense.

The objective of this phase is to evaluate the completed capstone project and determine whether the final manuscript satisfies the academic requirements for completion and archiving.

---

# Actors

* Coordinator
* Student (Capstone Group)
* Panelist

---

# Preconditions

Before this workflow begins:

* The Capstone 2 milestone is active.
* Chapters 4 and 5 have been approved by the assigned adviser.
* The capstone group is eligible for the Final Defense.

---

# Workflow Overview

The Final Defense follows the same workflow documented in the **Proposal Defense Workflow**, including:

* Defense scheduling
* Panel assignment
* Final manuscript submission
* Panel review and annotations
* Defense verdict
* Revision process (if required)
* Panel approval of revised submissions

---

# Additional Step – Archive Submission

After the Final Defense has been successfully completed:

1. The capstone group submits the approved final manuscript for archiving.

2. The system archives the approved manuscript.

3. The archived capstone project becomes available in the Repository for future reference.

---

# Postconditions

After successful completion:

* The Final Defense is completed.
* All required revisions have been approved.
* The final manuscript has been archived.
* The capstone project is marked as completed.

---

# Business Notes

* The Final Defense follows the same workflow as the Proposal Defense.
* Only approved final manuscripts may be archived.
* A capstone project is considered complete only after the approved manuscript has been successfully archived.
