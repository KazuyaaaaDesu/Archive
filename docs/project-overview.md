# Project Overview

## Archive

Archive is a web-based **Capstone Management System** developed for the **Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS)** program. The system is designed to digitalize and streamline the entire capstone process by providing a centralized platform where students, advisers, and coordinators can collaborate efficiently.

Traditionally, capstone projects involve manual document submissions, face-to-face consultations, and scattered communication and submission processes across multiple platforms such as Messenger, Google Drive, Microsoft Teams, and email. This fragmented workflow often leads to misplaced files, inconsistent document versions, delayed feedback, and difficulty tracking submissions and revisions throughout the capstone lifecycle.

The platform enables student groups to submit capstone documents, monitor their submission progress, receive adviser feedback, and manage revision requests. Advisers can review submissions, annotate documents, request revisions, approve chapters, and monitor the progress of their assigned groups. Coordinators oversee the entire capstone process by managing student groups, assigning advisers, monitoring submissions, scheduling defenses, and generating reports.

Archive emphasizes transparency, organization, and collaboration throughout the capstone lifecycle. Every submission, review, approval, and revision is recorded, allowing both students and faculty members to easily track the current status of each project.

The system is built using modern web technologies, including **Next.js**, **React**, **Prisma ORM**, **PostgreSQL**, and **CSS Modules**, providing a scalable, maintainable, and responsive application that follows current software development best practices.

## Project Goals

Archive aims to:

- Digitalize the capstone management process.
- Centralize workflow between students, advisers, and coordinators.
- Simplify document submission, review, and approval workflows.
- Improve progress tracking throughout the capstone lifecycle.
- Reduce paperwork, manual record keeping, and administrative overhead.
- Provide a secure, organized, and user-friendly platform for managing capstone projects.

## Target Users

The system is designed for five primary user roles:

- **Students** – Submit capstone documents, receive feedback, respond to revision requests, monitor project progress, and view announcements and schedules.

- **Advisers** – Review submissions, annotate documents, request revisions, approve chapters, and monitor the progress of their assigned groups.

- **Coordinators** – Manage the overall capstone process, oversee student groups and advisers, organize defense schedules, monitor project progress, and generate reports.

- **Program Chair** – Monitor the overall progress of capstone projects, review reports and analytics, and oversee the capstone program to ensure compliance with academic policies and program objectives.

- **Panelists** – Evaluate capstone projects during proposal and final defenses, provide comments and recommendations, and record evaluation results.
